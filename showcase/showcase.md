# Fathom

A little light. A very deep dark. You are a glowing forager in a flooded trench, eating your way through a maze you cannot see. Follow the tiny lights, remember the turns, and clear every mote to descend another depth.

Eating makes your light grow. It also makes you easier to find. Your circle opens onto remembered corridors, then closes behind you; beyond its edge, even familiar ground disappears into the abyss. Sonar reaches around corners to sketch a route through the darkness — but something out there is listening.

Three hunters read three different signals. The Lanternjaw follows your glow, disguised as a harmless amber jellyfish. The eyeless Gloamfin answers sound with a violet sweep of its own. The Flarefish vanishes between sudden blooms that burn through the rock. A sharp turn can buy time. A cloud of ink can break a light hunter’s fix. Neither makes you safe.

Swim, graze, and decide when knowing more is worth giving yourself away. There is always another corridor, another glimmer, another depth.
